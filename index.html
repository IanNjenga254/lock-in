RULES

Money management rules

✓
The 50/30/10/10 rule: 50% needs, 30% savings, 10% trading, 10% tools & self-investment
Automate transfers on payday. What leaves your account automatically gets saved.
✓
NEVER borrow money to trade. Ever. Under any circumstances.
Leverage + borrowed money = account blowup + debt. This rule has no exceptions.
✓
Build a 1-month expense emergency fund before touching trading capital
What is your monthly bare minimum expense? Multiply by 1.5. That's your target emergency fund.
✓
Reinvest 50% of freelance profits into tools, courses, and self-development in month 1–2
A Ksh 3,000 Udemy course that doubles your earning rate is a 10x return investment.

Explore Sacco membership — even student savings Saccos build credit history and loan eligibility
Stima Sacco, Mwalimu National, or university-affiliated Saccos. Long-term wealth tool.
