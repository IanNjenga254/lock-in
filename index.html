SMC CONCEPTS

Smart Money Concepts — your edge

ORDER BLOCKS (OB)

Last opposing candle before a strong impulsive move. Institutional accumulation/distribution zones. Mark bullish OB (last bearish candle before rally) and bearish OB (last bullish candle before drop).

FAIR VALUE GAPS (FVG)

3-candle imbalance where price moves too fast leaving unfilled orders. Price returns to fill these gaps. Mark the gap between candle 1 high and candle 3 low (bullish) or inverse (bearish).

BREAK OF STRUCTURE (BOS)

Price breaks a previous swing high (bullish BOS) or swing low (bearish BOS). Continuation signal. Higher highs + higher lows = bullish structure. Opposite = bearish.

CHANGE OF CHARACTER (ChoCh)

First BOS in the opposite direction after a trend — signals potential reversal. Wait for ChoCh, then look for entry on the retest of the OB that caused it.

LIQUIDITY SWEEPS

Price spikes to hunt stop losses above equal highs (buy-side liquidity) or below equal lows (sell-side liquidity) before reversing. Enter after the sweep, not before.

PREMIUM vs DISCOUNT

Use Fibonacci 0–1 range. Above 0.5 = premium (sell zone), below 0.5 = discount (buy zone). Only buy in discount, only sell in premium. This filters 80% of bad entries.

INDUCEMENT

False liquidity engineered by institutions to trap retail traders. Minor swing highs/lows before the actual target. Don't chase — wait for sweep of inducement then enter.

MITIGATION BLOCKS

Failed order blocks that get retested. When price breaks an OB and comes back, that's a mitigation. Can be stronger than standard OB because smart money is defending it again.
