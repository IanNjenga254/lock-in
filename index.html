SMC FRAMEWORK

Core Concepts

ORDER BLOCKS

Last opposing candle before a strong impulsive move. Bullish OB = last bearish candle before rally. Mark on H4+. This is institutional entry. Most reliable entry model in the framework.

FAIR VALUE GAPS

3-candle imbalance. Gap between candle 1 high and candle 3 low (bullish). Price returns to fill. Combine with OB for highest-confluence entries. FVG without OB confirmation is a lower-grade setup.

BOS / CHOCH

BOS = Break of Structure (trend continuation). ChoCh = Change of Character (first opposite BOS — potential reversal). Always track D1 first, confirm H4. Never bottom-up.

LIQUIDITY SWEEPS

Price spikes above equal highs (BSL) or below equal lows (SSL) to hunt stops, then reverses. Enter ONLY after the sweep candle closes back inside range. Never before the close.

PREMIUM vs DISCOUNT

Fib 0–1 range. Above 0.5 = premium (sell only). Below 0.5 = discount (buy only). This single filter eliminates 80% of bad entries. Non-negotiable on every single setup.

DISPLACEMENT

A strong impulsive candle creating an FVG and shifting market structure. Displacement = confirmation. Only enter after displacement in your direction. No displacement = no entry.

INDUCEMENT

False liquidity before the real target. Minor swing engineered to trap retail before the actual sweep. Wait for inducement to be swept THEN look for your entry. Patience here is the entire edge.

MITIGATION BLOCKS

Failed OBs that get retested. When price breaks an OB and returns, smart money defends it again. Often stronger entries than standard OBs because of double institutional interest at the zone.
