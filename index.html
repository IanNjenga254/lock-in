SMC FRAMEWORK

Smart Money Concepts — Core Concepts

ORDER BLOCKS

Last opposing candle before a strong impulsive move. Bullish OB = last bearish candle before rally. Mark on H4+. This is institutional entry. Most reliable entry model.

FAIR VALUE GAPS

3-candle imbalance. Gap between candle 1 high and candle 3 low (bullish). Price returns to fill. Combine with OB for highest-confluence entries.

BOS / CHOCH

BOS = Break of Structure (trend continuation). ChoCh = Change of Character (first opposite BOS, potential reversal). Track on D1 first, confirm H4.

LIQUIDITY SWEEPS

Price spikes above equal highs (BSL) or below equal lows (SSL) to hunt stops, then reverses. Enter after the sweep candle closes back inside. Never before.

PREMIUM vs DISCOUNT

Fib 0–1 range. Above 0.5 = premium (only sell). Below 0.5 = discount (only buy). This single filter eliminates 80% of bad entries. Non-negotiable.

DISPLACEMENT

A strong impulsive candle that creates an FVG and shifts market structure. Displacement is your confirmation signal. Enter only after displacement in your direction.

INDUCEMENT

False liquidity before the real target. Minor swing engineered to trap retail before the actual sweep. Wait for inducement to be swept THEN look for your entry.

MITIGATION BLOCKS

Failed OBs that get retested. When price breaks an OB and returns, smart money defends it again. Can be stronger entries than standard OBs because of double institutional interest.
